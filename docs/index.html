I · MISSION

Not another mysterious 83%.

Most pronunciation tools collapse a complex signal into a scalar. Gemellus aims for transparent diagnosis: what happened, where, why it differs from your chosen target, how certain the system is, and what to practice next.

Mensura ante iudiciumMeasure before judging. The LLM explains measured evidence; it does not invent phonetics.
Accent ≠ intelligibilityA legitimate accent is not an error. Target-accent deviation and communication problems are separate axes.
Pronunciation ≠ language choiceSegmentals, prosody, collocation, and grammar should remain separable so progress is interpretable.

III · WHAT WE WANT TO MEASURE

From vowels to victory.

Segmentals

Substitutions · insertions · deletions · consonant identity · vowel quality.

Vowel space

F1/F2/F3 distributions, normalization, duration and phonetic context.

Prosody

F0 contour · pitch range · stress placement · intensity · phrase boundaries.

Temporal fluency

Speech rate · articulation rate · pause count/location · syllable and phone duration.

Connected speech

Weak forms · schwa reduction · linking · tapping · aspiration/VOT · coarticulation where reliable.

Lexical naturalness

A separate transcript-based subsystem for collocations, word choice and discourse—not part of the pronunciation score.

VI · CENSOR'S OFFICE

How do we know it works?

The project should not declare itself accurate because its own score looks plausible. Evaluate against trained human raters and, eventually, against actual learning outcomes.

  • phone-level error detection
  • error-category agreement
  • word stress and prosody
  • overall intelligibility
  • similarity to the selected target accent
  • usefulness of generated feedback
  • longitudinal improvement after practice
VII · WHY “GEMELLUS”?

A Pannonian veteran with a very good project name.

Gemellus was a Pannonian auxiliary cavalryman whose surviving military diploma records Roman citizenship after a long military career in AD 122. The project borrows his journey as a playful metaphor for linguistic integration: acquiring command of another system without pretending your earlier identity never existed.

Historical metaphor only. We are training vowels, not invading Gaul. The maintainers have been advised accordingly.
